The preparation

Make the details work in your favor.

Every property and seller situation is different. These are the fundamentals to organize before bringing a home to market.

01

Understand the market

Compare relevant active and recently sold properties, location, condition, lot and construction areas, and current buyer demand.

02

Prepare the property

Gather plans and permits, service information, tax receipts, condominium details, and a clear record of improvements. Address presentation issues that affect first impressions.

03

Coordinate the process

Agree on the marketing plan, showing arrangements, inquiry handling, negotiation process, and qualified legal support for closing.

Seller checklist

Have the essentials ready.

Property title and identification details, recent predial and water receipts, construction plans and permits where applicable, condominium bylaws and fee status, utility information, and a list of included fixtures or furnishings.

For tax and closing implications, consult a qualified Mexican notario or tax professional before setting your final sale expectations. Marketing prices are not guaranteed sale prices.

Start with a conversation.

Share the neighborhood, property type, approximate size, and your preferred timing. We can discuss a sensible next step and, where needed, connect you with qualified local listing and transaction professionals.
