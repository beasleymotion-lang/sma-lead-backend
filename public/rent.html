A good rental fit

Look beyond the photographs.

Walk the route to the places you use most. Check noise, internet availability, water pressure, parking, stairs, sunlight, and how the property feels at different times of day.

01

Choose the area

Decide how close you want to be to Centro, cafés, schools, shops, and daily services.

02

Review the lease

Confirm rent, deposit, term, renewal, utilities, maintenance responsibilities, pets, and inventory in writing.

03

Document move-in

Photograph the condition, test appliances and utilities, record meter readings, and keep signed copies of the agreement.

Renting checklist

Before handing over a deposit.

Verify who is authorized to rent the property, review the complete lease, understand how the deposit is handled and returned, and obtain a written receipt for every payment. Avoid sending money before confirming the property and the person receiving funds.
