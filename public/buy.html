A more considered search

Buy with context, not guesswork.

San Miguel has historic homes, contemporary residences, gated communities, land, and properties that need renovation. The right choice depends on how you plan to use the property and what matters in daily life.

01

Define the brief

Set your budget, timing, intended use, must-haves, and preferred lifestyle before narrowing the search.

02

03

Review carefully

Have a qualified notario and independent legal and technical professionals review title, permits, liens, utilities, and the transaction documents.

Before you make an offer

Important questions for buyers.

Foreign ownership: San Miguel de Allende is inland and outside Mexico’s restricted coastal and border zones. Foreign buyers can generally hold title directly, subject to legal review and the specifics of the transaction.

Closing: A Mexican notario público plays a central role in formalizing the transaction. Ask for a written estimate of taxes, fees, and closing costs for the specific property.

Due diligence: Confirm title and liens, property tax and water accounts, construction and land-use permits, boundaries, condominium rules, and the condition of the home. Do not rely on listing information as a substitute for professional review.
